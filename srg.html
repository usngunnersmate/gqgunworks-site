SONORAN RECON GROUP

Sonoran Recon Group is a training collective, not a militia. It’s open to anyone who is legally allowed to possess firearms and wants to build useful skills, stay prepared, and train with good people.

The goal is simple: bring together veterans, active military, law enforcement, and civilians who want to learn, improve, and keep their skills sharp. Training includes tactics, evasion, survival, land navigation, shooting, close-quarters principles, long-range shooting, and TCCC, led by people with real experience in their fields.

The group is headed by a U.S. military veteran with nine years of service and multiple deployments with thousands of hours of extensive combat training including advanced combat shooting, counter IED, SERE, and, combat life saver . We have a focus on safe, practical, and useful training. We’ll also include optional workouts and physical training sessions for anyone wanting to improve their health and endurance.

We plan to meet one to three times per month depending on schedules. Some days will be serious training, some lighter, but the goal is always the same, stay ready, learn something new, and build a strong group of capable people.

It’s also about community. We want this to be a place to meet like-minded people, build friendships, and grab food or a beer afterward.
